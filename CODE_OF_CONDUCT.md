# Code of Conduct

Rowork is for people who want to make Roblox games, from a first project to a
studio. Everyone taking part in this project (issues, pull requests, discussions,
anywhere it is represented) is expected to help keep it welcoming.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with beginners. Questions that seem basic
  are welcome.
- Assume good faith, and disagree about ideas, not about people.
- Give and accept feedback graciously: review the change, not the author.
- Be honest about what you tested and what you did not.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language or jokes, whether aimed
  at a person or a group.
- Personal attacks, doxxing, or sharing someone's private information.
- Sexualised content, or unwelcome attention.
- Spam, or anything meant to mislead or harm other users, including malicious code
  or links.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may warn, temporarily limit or
permanently remove anyone who breaks these rules, when they judge it necessary to
protect the community.

To report a problem privately, use GitHub's private reporting on this repository
(*Security → Report a vulnerability* works for conduct reports too), or open an
issue asking a maintainer to contact you privately, without putting the details in
public. Reports are handled in confidence.

This code of conduct is inspired by widely used community standards such as the
Contributor Covenant.
